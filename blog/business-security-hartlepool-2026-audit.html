    Frequently Asked Questions

    How frequently should Hartlepool businesses audit their security systems?

    Commercial enterprises should conduct a formal, accredited security audit annually to maintain compliance with SSAIB, NSI, and commercial insurance policy conditions, or immediately following any structural building modifications.

    What is the difference between BS EN 50131 Grade 2 and Grade 3 alarms?

    Grade 2 alarm systems are designed to resist opportunistic intruders with basic tools. Grade 3 systems are engineered to combat sophisticated intruders equipped with advanced electronic bypass equipment, utilizing comprehensive anti-masking sensors mandatory for high-risk industrial facilities.

    Why is dual-path signaling necessary for commercial intruder alarms?

    Dual-path signaling utilizes two independent communication channels—typically a primary wired IP connection backed up by a dual-SIM cellular transmission path. If an intruder physically cuts your building lines, the cellular backup immediately transmits a confirmed line-fault alert to the Alarm Receiving Centre.
