## Who Is Gary Pearce?

Gary Pearce is an NSI and SSAIB certified security systems installer based in Northern England, specialising in CCTV installation, data cabling, alarm systems, and smart home security solutions.

With years of hands-on experience across Newcastle, Durham, Sunderland, Middlesbrough, Manchester, Leeds, and 30+ cities across the North, Gary has installed hundreds of security systems for homes, businesses, schools, care homes, retail shops, and industrial sites.

## Certifications & Standards

- **NSI Certified** — National Security Inspectorate approved installer
- **SSAIB Certified** — Security Systems and Alarms Inspection Board approved
- **ICO Registered** — Compliant with UK GDPR and data protection regulations
- **18th Edition Wiring Regulations** — BS 7671 compliant installations
