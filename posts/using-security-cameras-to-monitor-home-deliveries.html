Using Security Cameras to Monitor Home Deliveries

📅 Published: May 2026 ✅ Last updated: 23 May 2026 ⏱ 9 min read 🇬🇧 UK Home Guide
← All Guides
Direct Answer: To monitor home deliveries effectively, install a smart doorbell or dedicated security camera at a 45-degree angle to capture the package drop-off zone. Ensure your system features motion-triggered cloud recording and two-way audio to interact with couriers, providing a visual audit trail that discourages opportunistic package theft.

Why this topic matters & Core context

PoE (Power over Ethernet) cameras are essential for reliable delivery monitoring, as they ensure a constant power supply and stable data connection that avoids the signal drops common with battery-operated units. By maintaining a hard-wired link, you ensure that even during peak internet usage, your camera is ready to capture high-definition footage of every delivery.

As a professional installer, I recommend positioning your camera at eye level, roughly 1.5 to 1.8 meters high, to get a clear view of the courier's face and the package placement. This setup provides a superior vantage point compared to high-mounted cameras, which often suffer from 'top-down' distortion and obscure facial features.

Always prioritise cameras with 'Smart Person Detection' to reduce false alerts from swaying trees or passing vehicles.

Optimising Your View for Parcel Security

Field of view (FOV) is the most critical technical specification when selecting hardware for porch or driveway surveillance. A camera with at least a 140-degree wide-angle lens ensures that you capture both the doorstep and the surrounding area, preventing delivery drivers from leaving parcels in blind spots.

When installing, calibrate your motion zones within the app settings to focus exclusively on the path or porch area. This level of granularity prevents unnecessary push notifications while ensuring that as soon as a delivery vehicle arrives or a courier steps into your zone, the recording triggers instantly.

Best practice & Privacy compliance

GDPR compliance is vital when installing any residential security system that monitors public spaces like footpaths or neighbouring properties. You are legally required to ensure that your camera captures as little public land as possible, and placing a simple, visible sign stating that CCTV is in operation helps maintain transparency.

Regularly review your data retention settings to ensure you are not storing footage longer than necessary. Aligning your camera angle to face inward toward your own property boundaries keeps you on the right side of the law while still providing total visibility of your incoming deliveries.
