Using CCTV to Monitor Deliveries and Courier Access

📅 Published: May 2026 ✅ Last updated: 23 May 2026 ⏱ 9 min read 🇬🇧 UK Home Guide
← All Guides
Direct Answer: Monitoring courier access requires a high-resolution, wide-angle CCTV camera positioned at your doorstep or mailbox area. By utilizing PIR motion detection or AI-driven human recognition, you can receive real-time mobile notifications, verify deliveries instantly, and maintain a historical record of all package arrivals for your peace of mind.

Why this topic matters & Core context

Hikvision dome cameras and similar high-definition units have become the frontline of residential security, specifically for tracking courier activity. As online shopping continues to rise, homeowners face increased risks regarding parcel theft and delivery disputes, making visual verification an essential security component.

Proper installation involves mounting the camera at a height that allows for clear facial recognition while covering the entire porch area. I recommend utilizing Power-over-Ethernet (PoE) for stable, reliable transmission, ensuring the video feed remains uninterrupted when you need to review delivery timestamps.

Always ensure your camera's field of view is adjusted to avoid excessive capture of public footpaths to remain compliant with privacy regulations.

Technical Configuration for Delivery Alerts

AI-powered object detection is the gold standard for distinguishing delivery drivers from passing pedestrians or local wildlife. By configuring specific 'line-crossing' or 'intrusion detection' zones, you can eliminate false alerts triggered by wind or shadows in your driveway.

Commissioning these analytics requires a professional-grade NVR capable of processing metadata efficiently. Once set up, the system can trigger a mobile notification the moment a courier enters your defined zone, allowing you to view a live stream or communicate via two-way audio.

Best practice & Compliance

UK GDPR compliance dictates that you must be transparent about your use of surveillance if it captures areas beyond your own boundary. While you have the right to secure your property, signage is often recommended if your camera captures a shared driveway or a public road.

Regular maintenance, including lens cleaning and firmware updates, ensures that your security system continues to operate at peak efficiency. By keeping your security software patched, you protect your network from unauthorized access while ensuring your delivery records remain accessible only to you.
