1. AI Person & Vehicle Detection (AcuSense / Smart Detection)

AI-powered person and vehicle detection is essential for modern home security, as it drastically reduces false alarms by distinguishing real threats from wind, rain, and stray animals. Traditional motion detection triggered an alert whenever a pixel changed on screen — leading to hundreds of false alarms from swaying trees, rain, shadows, and moths.

Modern AI detection filters out these nuisances by identifying the specific shapes of humans and vehicles. You only get alerted when a real threat is present.

4. 4K Resolution for Crisp Detail

Opting for 4K Ultra-HD resolution guarantees incredibly sharp footage, allowing you to clearly identify faces, vehicle number plates, and small details at a distance. 4K resolution offers four times the detail of 1080p HD, delivering ultra-sharp images that can even distinguish between a person's facial features from over 30 meters away. This high-resolution footage is crucial for identifying suspects or license plates in case of an incident.

When purchasing a security camera system, look for models that support 4K resolution at 30 frames per second (fps) or higher to ensure smooth motion and clarity. Many advanced systems also offer intelligent zoom capabilities, allowing operators to digitally zoom in on areas of interest without losing quality.

Invest in a security camera system with 4K resolution for the clearest footage possible.

5. Colour Night Vision for Enhanced Visibility

Colour night vision transforms poor-light recording by capturing vital details like clothing and vehicle colours in pitch-black conditions, which standard black-and-white infrared systems miss. Colour night vision technology allows security cameras to capture full-colour images in complete darkness, using infrared LEDs to illuminate the scene without creating a glaring effect. This feature is essential for maintaining 24/7 surveillance and identifying individuals or objects accurately, even at night.

When selecting colour night vision cameras, look for models that offer adjustable IR sensitivity settings, allowing you to fine-tune the camera's performance based on your specific lighting conditions.

Choose security cameras with colour night vision capabilities for optimal visibility in low-light environments.

Local Storage Options for Data Security

Choosing local storage over subscription-only cloud plans ensures your private footage remains secure, readily accessible, and free from ongoing monthly costs. In addition to cloud storage, many modern security camera systems offer local storage options, such as built-in SD card slots or direct connections to network-attached storage (NAS) devices. Local storage provides an additional layer of data protection and ensures that your footage remains accessible even if internet connectivity is lost.

When selecting a security camera system with local storage capabilities, consider factors such as the maximum capacity of the included SD cards, the ease of swapping or expanding storage, and the ability to set up automated backup routines between cloud and local storage.

Opt for security cameras that offer both cloud and local storage options for enhanced data protection.

What is the difference between Cat6 and Cat6a cabling?
Cat6 supports speeds up to 10 Gbps up to 55 metres, while Cat6a supports 10 Gbps up to 100 metres and has better shielding. Read more in our Cat6 data cabling guide.
