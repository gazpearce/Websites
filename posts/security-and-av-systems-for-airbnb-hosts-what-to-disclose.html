Security and AV Systems for Airbnb Hosts: What to Disclose

📅 Published: May 2026 ✅ Last updated: 23 May 2026 ⏱ 9 min read 🇬🇧 UK Home Guide
← All Guides
Direct Answer: Airbnb hosts must disclose all security cameras and recording devices, including those that are turned off. You must specify their location in the listing description and never place them in private areas like bedrooms or bathrooms. Failure to comply violates Airbnb’s privacy policy, leading to account suspension or removal.

Why this topic matters & Core context

IP-based surveillance systems are now a standard expectation for property owners, but integrating them into short-term rentals requires navigating strict privacy boundaries. As a host, the hardware you choose—ranging from smart doorbells to professional internal monitors—must be balanced against the legal and ethical rights of your paying guests.

When installing these systems, ensure all outdoor cameras are clearly visible and documented in your listing. Avoid complex setups that monitor internal areas, as guest privacy is paramount; if an internal device is required for specific security needs, it must be explicitly disclosed and restricted from sensitive zones.

Always perform a full property walk-through to ensure your camera angles do not inadvertently overlook neighbouring gardens or private entrances.

Transparency and Platform Compliance

GDPR-compliant recording is not just a suggestion; it is a legal requirement in the UK when hosting transient guests. Transparency regarding your data capture methods, such as how long footage is stored on your NVR or cloud server, is critical to building a reputation as a professional host.

Your listing should detail the presence of all sensors and recording devices, including smart locks that track entry times. This technical transparency prevents disputes and ensures that your smart home integration remains a tool for safety rather than a liability.

Best practice for security integration

Hard-wired connectivity is the gold standard for reliable Airbnb security, offering a more stable and tamper-resistant alternative to consumer-grade Wi-Fi cameras. By using a robust network foundation, you can ensure that your security assets remain operational 24/7 without risking data dropouts or guest connectivity issues.

Optimise your system by using dedicated VLANs for guest devices and private security hardware to ensure total network isolation. By treating your Airbnb security infrastructure with the same rigour as a commercial installation, you safeguard your property and your guests’ peace of mind.
