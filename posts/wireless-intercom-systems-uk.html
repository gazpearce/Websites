Securing the perimeter of your property begins at the gate. A gate intercom lets you speak with visitors and release the lock remotely, keeping your property secure without needing to walk down the driveway. Wired intercoms are costly to install due to the trenching required for cables.

Why Install a Wireless Gate Intercom?

Wireless intercoms offer a cost-effective, clean alternative. In 2026, homeowners can choose between local radio-based systems, Wi-Fi smart doorbells, or mobile network (4G/5G) intercom systems that call your smartphone from anywhere.

DECT vs Wi-Fi vs 4G: Understanding Wireless Technologies

DECT (Digital Enhanced Cordless Telecommunications) intercoms operate on a dedicated 1.9GHz frequency, similar to cordless house phones. They provide excellent range (150–250m) and are immune to Wi-Fi interference. However, they only communicate with a dedicated indoor handset.

Wi-Fi intercoms connect to your home network, allowing you to answer the gate from your phone. They are highly convenient but require a strong, stable Wi-Fi signal at the gate. 4G GSM intercoms use a SIM card to dial your mobile phone directly. They have unlimited range but require a monthly cellular subscription.

Choose DECT for simple gate-to-house calling. Choose 4G if you want to answer gate calls when away from home.

Integrating Gate Releases and Smart Locks

An intercom is only half the solution; you also need a way to open the gate. Most wireless intercom transmitters include a relay switch that connects to the gate controller's open/close terminals.

When you press the key button on your indoor handset or smartphone app, it triggers the relay, sending a low-voltage pulse to release the electric strike or activate the gate motor. Ensure your gate controller has backup battery storage in case of power cuts.

Overcoming Signal Obstacles & Weatherproofing

Stone walls, metal gates, and wet foliage block wireless signals. If installing a Wi-Fi intercom at a metal gate, the gate itself can act as a shield, completely blocking the signal. Install the antenna on a brick pillar next to the gate instead.

The outdoor call station must have an IP65 or IP66 weatherproof rating to withstand UK winters. Use silicon sealant around the backplate to prevent water ingress behind the call button, which can corrode the battery terminals.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I get Wi-Fi signal down to my driveway gate?
You can extend your network using a dedicated outdoor wireless access point mounted on the house wall, or run an outdoor-rated cable to the gate. For cabling guidelines, check our running ethernet to a garden office guide.
Can I connect my gate intercom to a smart home system?
Yes, Wi-Fi intercoms like Ring or Nest can integrate with Google Home or Amazon Alexa, allowing you to view the gate camera on smart screens. Learn about integrations in our smart home installation guide.
Do wireless gate intercoms need professional installation?
Battery-powered units are simple DIY projects, but connecting the intercom relay to an electric gate motor usually requires professional wiring. Check security install guidelines in our burglar alarm installation guide.
Do smart doorbells work as gate intercoms?
Only if the gate is close to the house. Smart doorbells suffer from high latency and connection dropouts at distances over 15 metres. Check our Ring doorbell installation guide for distance tips.
What is the best material for a gate call station?
Marine-grade stainless steel or powder-coated aluminium is best. Avoid cheap plastics which degrade under UV exposure and crack in sub-zero temperatures. Check our CCTV maintenance guide for hardware care.
