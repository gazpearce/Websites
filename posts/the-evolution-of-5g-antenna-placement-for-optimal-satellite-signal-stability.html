Why this topic matters & Core context

Radio frequency interference (RFI) has become a critical challenge for homeowners as 5G networks expand, often conflicting with legacy satellite infrastructure. This overlap can cause significant packet loss and signal dropouts, which is particularly disastrous if your security system relies on a stable satellite connection for off-site alarm monitoring.

As a specialist, I recommend conducting a comprehensive RF survey before mounting any new 5G hardware. Properly spacing your antennas away from satellite dishes and utilizing high-quality, double-shielded coaxial cables are essential initial steps for any professional-grade security installation.

Always verify your local base station proximity before installing high-gain antennas to avoid saturating your satellite receiver's front-end.

Technical mitigation strategies

Band-pass filters represent the industry standard for isolating satellite LNB signals from the encroaching 5G spectrum. By installing these filters directly at the LNB entry point, you can effectively block the specific frequencies used by modern mobile networks while allowing your satellite signal to pass through unhindered.

Precision in antenna orientation is equally vital. Ensuring that your 5G antenna is mounted at an orthogonal angle to the satellite dish helps minimize direct signal interference, preserving the integrity of your security footage transmission and overall home network stability.

Best practice & compliance

Professional cable certification is the final stage of ensuring a robust, interference-free installation. Using inferior cabling in an environment saturated with high-frequency signals often negates the benefits of proper antenna placement, leading to intermittent connectivity issues in your CCTV system.

Compliance with local planning regulations and the latest UK telecommunications standards is non-negotiable. Always ensure your installation adheres to current guidance on equipment placement, as this not only maintains signal stability but also ensures your insurance remains valid in the event of a security failure.
