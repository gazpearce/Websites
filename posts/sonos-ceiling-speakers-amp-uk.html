Ceiling speakers provide a clean, minimalist audio solution, eliminating speaker cabinets and messy cables from your living space. By powering these speakers with a Sonos Amp, you integrate them natively into the Sonos multi-room ecosystem, allowing you to stream music via Apple AirPlay 2, Spotify, or the Sonos app.

Why Choose Sonos Ceiling Speakers?

The Sonos Amp delivers 125 watts per channel at 8 ohms, which is more than enough to drive high-quality ceiling speakers. When paired with Sonos Architectural speakers (by Sonance), the system supports Trueplay tuning, which calibrates the audio output to match your room's acoustics.

Wiring Routes & Cable Specifications (16/2 vs 14/2)

All ceiling speakers are passive and require physical speaker cables routed back to the Sonos Amp location. For cable runs under 25 metres, standard 16-gauge, 2-core (16/2) oxygen-free copper speaker cable is ideal.

For runs over 25 metres, upgrade to 14-gauge (14/2) cable to prevent signal loss and maintain bass response. Always choose LSZH (Low Smoke Zero Halogen) rated cables to comply with UK fire regulations for cable runs behind plasterboard walls.

Never run speaker cables parallel to mains electrical wiring; cross them at 90-degree angles to avoid hum.

UK Building Regulations: Installing Fire Hoods

Cutting a hole in a plasterboard ceiling compromises the ceiling's fire barrier. In the UK, Building Regulations (Part B) require that if you install a ceiling speaker beneath a habitable room, you must install an approved speaker fire hood over the back of the speaker.

In the event of a fire, the hood expands to seal the speaker cutout, preventing fire and smoke from spreading into the floor void above for up to 60 minutes. Fire hoods also improve audio quality by stopping sound leaking into the rooms above.

Connecting Multiple Speakers in Parallel

A single Sonos Amp can power multiple speakers. If you are using Sonos by Sonance Architectural speakers, the Amp can drive up to three pairs (six speakers total) wired in parallel.

If you are using third-party speakers (like KEF, Monitor Audio, or B&W), the Amp can safely drive two pairs (four speakers total) at 8 ohms. Wiring more than this will drop the impedance below 4 ohms, causing the Amp to overheat and trigger its protection circuit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I connect my TV to a Sonos Amp?
Yes. The Sonos Amp features an HDMI ARC input, allowing you to connect it to your TV. The Amp will decode the audio and power your ceiling speakers as front left and right channels. Learn about mounting TVs in our TV wall mounting guide.
Do ceiling speakers require fire hoods in single-storey extensions?
If there is no habitable room above (e.g. only a loft space or flat roof), fire hoods are not strictly required by building codes, but they are still recommended for acoustic insulation. Learn about building codes in our CCTV standards guide.
How do I group ceiling speakers with a Sonos Arc soundbar?
You can configure the Sonos Amp to act as surround channels. The Amp will bond wirelessly to your Sonos Arc or Beam soundbar over Wi-Fi. Check out our whole home audio guide for system layouts.
What is the best distance between ceiling speakers?
For optimal stereo imaging, space speakers between 2 and 3 metres apart, and keep them at least 0.5 metres away from side walls. Check network layouts in our home network setup guide.
Can I run speaker cable outside to garden speakers?
Yes, but you must use UV-resistant, external-grade speaker cable to prevent the outer sheath cracking. Learn about outdoor cabling in our outdoor ethernet installation guide.
