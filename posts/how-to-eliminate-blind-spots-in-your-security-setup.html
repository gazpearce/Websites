How to Eliminate Blind Spots in Your Security Setup

📅 Published: May 2026 ✅ Last updated: 23 May 2026 ⏱ 9 min read 🇬🇧 UK Home Guide
← All Guides
Direct Answer: Eliminate security blind spots by conducting a professional site survey, utilizing overlapping fields of view between cameras, and integrating motion-activated sensors to cover peripheral zones. Position your hardware at strategic heights and angles to ensure total coverage of entry points, driveways, and vulnerable corridors around your property.

Why this topic matters & Core context

Hikvision dome cameras and similar high-resolution units are highly effective, yet even the most expensive gear fails if your placement strategy is flawed. A security system with gaps provides a false sense of security, allowing intruders to navigate your property undetected by simply stepping into the 'dark' areas between camera angles.

To mitigate this, I always conduct a physical site survey during the planning phase to map out exact dead zones. We use a 'cross-coverage' technique where each camera's detection range slightly overlaps with the next, ensuring that no movement goes unrecorded regardless of the intruder's path.

Always prioritize 15% overlap between your camera detection zones to ensure continuous coverage across your entire perimeter.

Advanced hardware and strategic positioning

Panoramic dual-lens security cameras have revolutionized how we secure residential estates by reducing the total number of hardware units required while maximizing field-of-view efficiency. By deploying these wide-angle devices, you can eliminate the narrow gaps typically found when using multiple fixed-lens cameras, resulting in a cleaner and more effective security footprint.

Mounting height is equally critical; cameras placed too low are vulnerable to vandalism, while those too high create severe downward blind spots directly beneath the unit. We recommend mounting at 2.5 to 3 meters to balance optimal facial recognition clarity with a wide enough angle to minimize the immediate proximity blind spot.

Best practice & environmental integration

AI-driven line crossing detection serves as the final line of defense, acting as a secondary verification layer that monitors areas your cameras might only capture partially. When combined with smart motion sensors, this technology alerts you the moment movement is detected in a blind spot, effectively bridging the gap between hardware limitations and total property visibility.

Compliance with UK privacy standards means you must document your field of view to ensure you are not infringing on neighbors' private land, even while trying to cover your own blind spots. Keep an updated site plan that balances your need for security with ethical surveillance practices, ensuring you remain fully compliant while tightening your home's defenses.
